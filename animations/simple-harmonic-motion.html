Understanding SHM

Simple harmonic motion is a repeated oscillation about an equilibrium position.

The restoring acceleration is proportional to the displacement and is directed towards equilibrium: a = −ω²x.

At the extreme positions, displacement is maximum and velocity is zero. At equilibrium, displacement is zero and velocity is maximum.
