What is happening?

In simple harmonic motion, the particle oscillates repeatedly about an equilibrium position.

The restoring acceleration is always directed towards the equilibrium position and is proportional to the displacement: a = −ω²x.

At the extreme positions, displacement is maximum and velocity becomes zero. At equilibrium, velocity is maximum while displacement is zero.
