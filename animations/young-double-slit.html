Learn: Young's Double-Slit Experiment
When light passes through two very close slits, the waves from the slits overlap. They can reinforce each other or cancel each other. This produces a pattern of bright and dark fringes on the screen.
Constructive interference Waves meet in phase, giving maximum intensity and a bright fringe.
Destructive interference Waves meet out of phase, reducing the intensity and producing a dark fringe.
Fringe width β The distance between two consecutive bright fringes or two consecutive dark fringes.
Try it: Increase the wavelength λ → fringes become farther apart. Increase the slit separation d → fringes become closer together. Increase the screen distance D → fringes become farther apart.
