# IDEA LAB

### Interactive Physics • Visual Experiments • Exploration

IDEA LAB is an interactive physics laboratory designed to make physics easier to explore through visual simulations and experiments.

Instead of only reading equations and diagrams, users can interact with simulations, change experimental conditions, and observe how the system responds.

---

## 🔬 Simulations

### 🌊 Wave Propagation Simulation in 3D
Explore how waves propagate through three-dimensional space.

**Controls**
- Amplitude
- Wavelength
- Frequency
- Phase

[Open Simulation](animations/wave-propagation-3d.html)

---

### 🔬 Young's Double-Slit Experiment
Explore the interference of coherent light and observe the formation of bright and dark fringes.

**Controls**
- Wavelength
- Slit separation
- Screen distance

[Open Simulation](animations/young-double-slit.html)

---

### ⚙️ Simple Harmonic Motion
Visualize oscillatory motion using an interactive spring-mass system.

Explore displacement, velocity and acceleration while observing the motion directly.

[Open Simulation](animations/simple-harmonic-motion.html)

---

## 🧠 What IDEA LAB Focuses On

IDEA LAB currently explores physics through:

- Waves
- Optics
- Interference
- Mechanics
- Electricity
- Modern Physics

The project is designed around a simple idea:

> **Learn physics by seeing it happen.**

## 🤖 IDEA LAB Assistant

IDEA LAB also includes an AI-powered assistant that can help users:

- Understand physics concepts
- Find simulations
- Learn how to use the available experiments
- Navigate the website

The assistant is designed specifically around the content and features available in IDEA LAB.
